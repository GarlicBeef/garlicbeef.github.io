The paper reports an interaction-induced, controllable deformation of the Fermi surface in a deeply degenerate
Fermi gas of ²³Na⁴⁰K molecules. Double microwave shielding reduces inelastic losses about threefold compared with
single shielding while keeping strong elastic dipolar scattering. Fermi surface deformations of up to 7% are
observed, and tuning the interaction from axial U(1) to biaxial C₂ symmetry imprints that geometry onto the Fermi
surface. The measurements agree with parameter-free Hartree–Fock theory.
