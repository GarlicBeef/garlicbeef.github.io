Using Rydberg blockade and facilitation in an atom array, the Hilbert space is fragmented into exponentially many
disjoint subspaces. The experiment shows quantum many-body scars from ℤ₂ₖ initial states and thermalization that
remains restricted to individual subspaces: states in different subspaces do not thermalize with one another even
at the same energy.
